# Exam map

This track prepares you for **Claude Certified Architect – Foundations (CCAR-F)**, Anthropic's proctored exam for architects who design agent systems. The developer exam, CCDV-F, and the free Claude Academy courses have separate tracks.

## Who can sit the exams

Only employees of companies in the **Claude Partner Network** can certify, and registration needs a partner email on a recognised company domain. Customers and non-partners are not eligible yet. Partner Network membership is free for organizations.

The free **Claude Academy** courses award completion badges independently of exam eligibility.

## Exam facts

- **Time:** 120 minutes of testing; plan for about 135 minutes in the seat.
- **Format:** multiple choice and scenario-based multiple response, closed book, English only.
- **Delivery:** Pearson OnVUE online proctoring or a Pearson test center.
- **Score:** scaled from 100 to 1,000; **720 passes** for this guide.
- **Retakes:** wait 14 days, then 30, then 90; at most 4 attempts per exam in a rolling 12 months, full fee each time.
- **Validity:** 12 months. On-time renewal is a free, unproctored assessment; a lapse means retaking the full exam.

## CCAR-F domains and the six scenarios

| Domain | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Agentic architecture and orchestration | 27% |
| Claude Code configuration and workflows | 20% |
| Prompt engineering and structured output | 20% |
| Tool design and MCP integration | 18% |
| Context management and reliability | 15% |

Each sitting draws **4 of 6** published production scenarios, with roughly 15 questions each: customer support resolution agent, code generation with Claude Code, multi-agent research system, developer productivity with Claude, Claude Code for CI/CD, and structured data extraction. The scenarios overlap in the skills they test.
