# Exam map & study plan

This track prepares you for **Claude Certified Architect – Foundations (CCAR-F)**, Anthropic's proctored exam for architects who design agent systems. Study the material even if you cannot sit the exam yet: it is exactly what senior AI-agent interviews probe. New to Claude? Start with the free Claude 101 and Claude Code 101 tiles. The developer exam, CCDV-F, has its own tile.

## Who can sit the exams

Only employees of companies in the **Claude Partner Network** can certify, and registration needs a partner email on a recognised company domain. Customers and non-partners are not eligible yet. Partner Network membership is free for organizations, so the first question to ask is whether your employer is already a partner.

If you are not eligible, the free **Claude Academy** courses award completion badges that anyone can earn: after the 101 courses, take Claude Code in Action, Building with the Claude API, Introduction to Model Context Protocol, MCP Advanced Topics, and Introduction to Agent Skills.

## Exam facts to memorise

- **Time:** 120 minutes of testing; plan for about 135 minutes in the seat.
- **Format:** multiple choice and scenario-based multiple response, closed book, English only.
- **Delivery:** Pearson OnVUE online proctoring or a Pearson test center.
- **Score:** scaled from 100 to 1,000; **720 passes** for this guide.
- **Retakes:** wait 14 days, then 30, then 90; at most 4 attempts per exam in a rolling 12 months, full fee each time.
- **Validity:** 12 months. On-time renewal is a free, unproctored assessment; a lapse means retaking the full exam.
- **Readiness:** a practice result is diagnostic, not a guaranteed pass or an officially established booking threshold.

## CCAR-F domains and the six scenarios

| Domain | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Agentic architecture and orchestration | 27% |
| Claude Code configuration and workflows | 20% |
| Prompt engineering and structured output | 20% |
| Tool design and MCP integration | 18% |
| Context management and reliability | 15% |

Each sitting draws **4 of 6** published production scenarios, with roughly 15 questions each: customer support resolution agent, code generation with Claude Code, multi-agent research system, developer productivity with Claude, Claude Code for CI/CD, and structured data extraction. Study all six; they overlap heavily in the skills they test.

## How to use this track

1. Earn the free Claude 101 and Claude Code 101 badges; their tiles have study notes and practice quizzes.
2. Read the five domain chapters in order.
3. Do at least three of the hands-on labs; hands-on time is what separates a pass from a near miss.
4. Take the 40-question mock exam under an 80-minute timer.
5. Use the final review's distractor patterns to explain every wrong answer.

A realistic pace is four weeks at about an hour a day.

Architecture: classify done/error/budget traces. Tools: reject an unapproved cross-tenant write. Prompts: distinguish JSON shape, business validity and source support. Code: inspect a minimal diff and independent tests. Context: preserve sourced case facts across a handoff. These offline cases support the guide tasks but do not establish production readiness; use the current official guide for the complete objectives.
