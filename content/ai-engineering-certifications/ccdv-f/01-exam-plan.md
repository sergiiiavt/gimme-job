# CCDV-F exam map & study plan

**Claude Certified Developer – Foundations (CCDV-F)** is Anthropic's proctored exam for engineers who build on the Claude API. It is a separate exam from the architect exam, CCAR-F. CCDV-F weights integration, model choice and API mechanics most, while CCAR-F weights agent architecture and Claude Code configuration.

## Who can sit it

Only employees of companies in the **Claude Partner Network** can certify, and registration needs a partner email on a recognised company domain. Partner Network membership is free for organizations, so first find out whether your employer is a partner. If you are not eligible yet, study this track anyway: it is what LLM-integration interviews ask about. In the meantime, the free Claude Platform 101 badge is open to anyone.

## Exam facts to memorise

- **Questions:** 53, as reported by prep providers; Anthropic does not publish the count.
- **Price:** USD 125.
- **Time:** 120 minutes of testing; plan for about 135 minutes in the seat.
- **Format:** multiple choice and scenario-based multiple response, closed book, English only.
- **Delivery:** Pearson OnVUE online proctoring or a Pearson test center.
- **Score:** scaled from 100 to 1,000; **720 passes**.
- **Retakes:** wait 14 days, then 30, then 90; at most 4 attempts in a rolling 12 months, full fee each time.
- **Validity:** 12 months. On-time renewal is a free, unproctored assessment; a lapse means retaking the full exam.

## How to use this track

1. If the API is new to you, earn the free Claude Platform 101 badge first; it covers the basics this track assumes.
2. Read the four domain chapters in order. Half the exam sits in the first two.
3. Do at least two of the three hands-on labs.
4. Take the 30-question mock exam under a 60-minute timer.
5. Use the final review to explain every wrong answer before you retake it.

A realistic pace is three to four weeks at about an hour a day.
