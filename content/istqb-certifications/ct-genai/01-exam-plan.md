# Exam map

This track prepares you for **ISTQB Certified Tester – Testing with Generative AI (CT-GenAI) v1.1**, the ISTQB specialist certificate for using generative AI in testing. It complements CT-AI: CT-AI covers testing AI-based systems, while CT-GenAI covers using LLMs to do testing work.

## Exam facts

- **Syllabus:** v1.1, effective 27 April 2026, a minor update to v1.0.
- **Questions:** 40 multiple-choice questions.
- **Pass mark:** 65%.
- **Time:** 60 minutes, plus 25% when the exam is not in your native language.
- **Prerequisite:** ISTQB Certified Tester Foundation Level (CTFL). No programming experience is required.
- **Validity:** the certificate does not expire.
- **Official practice:** sample exam A with answers for v1.1.

## Cognitive and hands-on levels

Learning objectives use **K1 Remember**, **K2 Understand** and **K3 Apply**. K3 objectives include GenAI-2.2.1–2.2.5 (applied prompting) and GenAI-3.1.2 (identify risks in a GenAI testing scenario). The syllabus also has hands-on objectives from H0 (watch a demo) to H2 (exercise with hints); they build skill but are not examined as practical tasks.

## The five chapters

| Chapter | Topic | Minimum training time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Introduction to generative AI for software testing | 100 min |
| 2 | Prompt engineering for effective software testing | 365 min |
| 3 | Managing risks of generative AI in software testing | 160 min |
| 4 | LLM-powered test infrastructure | 110 min |
| 5 | Deploying and integrating GenAI in test organisations | 80 min |

Chapter 2 is almost half the training time and holds most applied-prompting objectives; risk identification in chapter 3 is also K3. Training time describes the syllabus allocation, not the question distribution of a particular exam.

## Applied-objective coverage

GenAI-2.2.1–2.2.5 cover prompt construction, test analysis, design/implementation, regression analysis and monitoring/control. GenAI-3.1.2 covers risk identification in a GenAI testing workflow. H-level exercises are learning activities, not practical exam tasks.
