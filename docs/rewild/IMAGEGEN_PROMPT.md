# Vertical-slice target image prompt

## Use case

Stylized concept art. This image is the authoritative production target for the first Rewild vertical slice, not a shippable gameplay background.

## Reference roles

- `approved-world-direction.png`: quality, density, color, pixel-art finish, natural transitions, and object integration.
- `baseline-before-vertical-slice.png`: current camera, approximate composition, central house, curved road, and two-pond layout.

## Prompt

Create an authoritative 16:9 gameplay-scale visual target for a handcrafted 2D pixel-art ecological tower-defense map. Preserve the broad camera and composition of the current game reference: a central wooden house, a curved road across the lower third, two ponds, and open traversal space. Raise the visual quality, density, natural transitions, color depth, and object integration to match the approved world-direction reference.

The world must read as one connected place. On the left and center, show a healthy living meadow with clustered old trees, shrubs, flower patches, stones, roots, reeds, fences, small garden beds, and paths that physically meet the house and road. Avoid empty flat grass and repetitive tiling.

In the upper-right and right side, show a large modular datacenter compound that is visibly built into the environment: excavated ground, concrete foundations, connected server and cooling modules, fenced utilities, transformers, crates, a loading area, drains, pipes, and thick power/data cables that enter and leave the structures. The datacenter must occupy multiple connected modules rather than read as one icon.

Show a continuous environmental reaction around it: healthy green meadow becomes yellow stressed grass, then compacted dirt, cracked contaminated soil, dark sludge, dead plants, polluted runoff, and debris. One pond should be clean and alive; the pond near the datacenter should be visibly polluted by a connected drain or runoff channel. Corruption must reshape the ground, not appear as a translucent overlay.

Include one small readable combat relationship near the transition zone: defenders anchored in vegetation or cover, enemies emerging from cables, sludge, or damaged infrastructure, projectiles or impact pixels, and localized ground response. Keep animation implied through state and action, not through global wobble or excessive particles.

Use authentic handcrafted pixel art with crisp nearest-neighbor edges, coherent sprite scale, colored dark outlines, upper-left lighting, restrained ambient occlusion, irregular organic silhouettes, and a rich but unified green-earth-industrial palette. No visible hex grid, no square grid, no flat rectangular terrain patches, no large empty lawn, no oval shadow under the house, no floating objects, no UI, no labels, no text, and no watermark.
